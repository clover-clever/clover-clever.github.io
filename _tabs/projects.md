## RACE Hanyang

**2022–2026 · Formula Student EV**

I worked on the electrical and high-voltage systems for [RACE Hanyang](https://www.racehanyang.com/), Hanyang University's Formula Student team. My work covered the battery pack and BMS, safety circuits, wiring, and interfaces between the inverter and vehicle controllers.

I also served as electrical lead and later as team president. The cars were a team effort, built by people across all the different subteams.

[Team website](https://www.racehanyang.com/)

## NuBiHo Quad Drone

**2025 · Microprocessor course project**

For a university course, I used an Arduino Uno R3 to generate PPM signals for a quadcopter flight controller. I also made custom PCBs and ran into a few power-supply problems along the way. The repo has notes on what went wrong.

[GitHub repository](https://github.com/clover-clever/NuBiHo_Quad_Drone)
