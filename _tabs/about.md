**SangHoon Lee**  
Automotive Engineering, Hanyang University

I'm an undergraduate interested in motor control, power electronics, and embedded systems.

I joined [RACE Hanyang](https://www.racehanyang.com/) in 2022 and worked on the electrical and high-voltage systems for our Formula Student EVs. I later led the electrical team and served as team president.

After spending years building the cars, I've been digging deeper into how motor drives and inverters work. There's still a lot I want to learn.

## Interests

- **Motor Control:** PMSMs, current control, motor drives
- **Power Electronics:** inverters, power converters
- **Embedded Systems:** STM32, real-time control
- **Other interests:** math, physics, drones
